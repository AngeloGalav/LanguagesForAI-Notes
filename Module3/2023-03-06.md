### 1. Excercise on asymptotic notation. 
Since $log^2()$ function grows even slower than $log()$, we can conclude that $f_1$ is growing asymptotically faster than $g_1$. 
We can then conclude that: 
- $f_1(n) \in \Omega(g_1(n))$
- $f_1(n) \not \in O(g_1(n))$
- $f_1(n) \not \in \Theta(g_1(n))$
- $g_1(n) \in O(f_1(n))$
